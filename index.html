What the model should eventually know

A good forecast needs more than school prestige. The strongest version should learn what was knowable before each contest.

  • Recent national and regional scores, normalized by season and stage
  • Opponent strength and path difficulty
  • Round-specific performance: speed, problem solving, true/false and riddles
  • Returning contestants and substitutions
  • Recent consistency, variance and comeback rate
  • Question-topic exposure and subject balance, when transcripts are verified
  • Seeding and prior-year depth, with decay for older seasons

Research method

Data quality is part of the product.

If two reports disagree, the app should show the conflict instead of quietly choosing whichever value looks convenient.

1. Primary first

Official NSMQ articles, official videos and the official winners archive sit at the top of the evidence hierarchy.

2. Verify gaps

Trusted Ghanaian media can fill older gaps. Video becomes the deciding source when score reports conflict or contestant names are incomplete.

3. Prevent leakage

Forecast features must be timestamped. A model predicting a quarter-final cannot learn from that school’s semi-final or final result from the same season.
